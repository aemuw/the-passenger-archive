# Real stories — research notes

Status: **research only, nothing here is in the game yet.** The `RealWorld` texts in `Carriages.cs` are still placeholders.
Rules for every DOCUMENTED / INSPIRED carriage: facts only from the sources below (or better ones), names of victims
changed, appearance invented, the real case named only on the final card, never as a reconstruction that exploits a person.

## ⚠ Design warning: the "rule" mechanic can blame victims
"Break the rule → you die the way the victim died" teaches the player that the victim did something wrong.
For fictional carriages that is fine. For real crimes it is dangerous: after the Sarah Everard case, advice aimed at
potential victims was widely criticised as shifting responsibility onto them (see sources 1–3). Recommendation:
in DOCUMENTED carriages the player is **never punished for the victim's "mistake"**. The hidden rule there should be about
what *bystanders, institutions or neighbours* could have done, or about equipment/environment — not about the victim's choices.

## Candidates

### A. Carbon monoxide (no villain, no victim-blaming) — best first DOCUMENTED carriage
Ordinary winter evening, a stove / gas boiler / generator, a family asleep. Fear = how ordinary it is.
Hidden rule born from the memory: the small "this is fine" signs (headache, drowsiness, the generator moved indoors "just for tonight").
Facts (State Emergency Service of Ukraine / Ministry of Health, via press): in November 2022, 368 people were poisoned by
carbon monoxide in Ukraine, 92 of them children, 20 died; in the first week of December 2022 — 97 poisoned, 14 died;
over two winter months of 2023 DSNS recorded 800+ poisonings. Main causes named by DSNS: wrong use of alternative
heating/power sources (generators, stoves), faulty chimneys/boilers.
- Sources: gazeta.ua/articles/np/_sotni-ukraincev-otravilis-ugarnym-gazom-kakaya-glavnaya-prichina/1124753 ·
  espreso.tv (97 poisoned, 14 died, first week of Dec) · slovoidilo.ua/2023/01/27/… (800+) · suspilne.media/343412-… (DSNS advice).
- Format suggestion: several people in one carriage, **no killer**, the "conductor" says only the numbers at the end.

### B. Suzy Lamplugh (UK, 1986) — "no good explanation" carriage, no killer on the train
25-year-old estate agent, vanished on 28 July 1986 while meeting a client listed in her diary as "Mr Kipper".
Never found. **No one has been convicted of her murder** (a suspect, John Cannan, was never charged for it) — so the game must
not show any "killer" for this case. Her parents founded the Suzy Lamplugh Trust (December 1986), which created
the discipline of personal-safety training; the Trust later published "Suzy's Code" for estate agents.
Lesson at the system level: lone working, telling colleagues who/where. Fits the "some stories have no answer" type.
- Sources: swlondoner.co.uk (cold case reopened; trust statement) · shponline.co.uk (30 years since) ·
  estateagenttoday.co.uk (Suzy's Code, 2018 obituary of her father) · scotsman.com (obituary of Diana Lamplugh).

### C. Sarah Everard (UK, 2021) — handle with extreme care
Convicted (whole-life order): a serving police officer used a false arrest (warrant card, handcuffs, pretext of Covid rules)
to put her in his car. Witnesses assumed an undercover arrest. The Met then advised people who doubt a lone plain-clothes
officer to ask where their colleagues are, to speak to the control room, and said plain-clothes officers would no longer
work alone. The advice was heavily criticised as irrelevant when the danger is the officer himself.
Use only if the carriage is about **the system and the bystanders** (the couple who assumed it was legitimate; the institution),
never about "what she should have done".
- Sources: stylist.co.uk/news/dealing-with-police-rights/572290 · marieclaire.co.uk (…750814) ·
  staffordshire-live.co.uk (…6002671, …6009450) · lbc.co.uk (…DWz58r_2).
